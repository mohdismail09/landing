The problem

Your counter staff are retyping orders that already exist.

Every PO a customer emails gets keyed in again, line by line. It's slow, it's error-prone, and it gets worse when you're busiest.

Hours lost to retyping

A long PO can take half an hour to key in. That's time your people could spend with customers or chasing quotes.

Typos and wrong parts

One wrong digit ships the wrong fitting. Then come the return, the re-delivery and the unhappy contractor.

Clerks are hard to keep

Hiring and training an order clerk takes months. When they leave, you start over.

Busy season backs up

When HVAC season hits or a big job starts, orders pile up. Customers wait, and some call a competitor.

How it works

Four steps. Your customers only see the first one.

No new portal, no new forms. We work from the orders you already receive.

  1. 01

    Customers send orders as usual

    Email, PDF, scanned fax or spreadsheet. You forward them to us, or we read a shared inbox.

  2. 02

    AI reads and matches every line

    Each line is matched to your catalog, using your part numbers, units and the customer's past orders.

  3. 03

    Our reviewers check anything uncertain

    If the AI isn't sure, a trained person on our team decides. Lines we can't resolve are flagged for you.

  4. 04

    Orders wait in your ERP for approval

    Clean draft orders are in your system by morning. Nothing ships without your OK.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Something else on your mind? Email ismail@mohdismail.com.

How accurate is it?

That depends on your catalog and your customers' orders, so we'd rather show you than quote a number. The free 20-order test shows exactly how we handle your real orders before you pay anything. Every line the AI isn't sure about is checked by a person, and you approve every order.

How fast do orders come back?

Orders received by 6 pm your time are usually waiting in your ERP by 8 am the next morning. If you need faster turnaround for rush orders, we can set that up with you.

What happens if the AI gets a line wrong?

When the AI isn't confident, a trained reviewer on our team checks the line. If we still can't be sure, for example a part you don't stock, we flag it instead of guessing. Every order lands as a draft, so you review it before anything ships.

Which ERPs do you work with?

We deliver orders in the import format for Prophet 21, Eclipse, NetSuite, Acumatica and QuickBooks. If your system can import a CSV file, we can work with it too.

How long does setup take?

You send us your catalog export and some recent orders, and set up email forwarding. We handle the rest, and walk you through it during the free test.

Do I have to sign a long contract?

No. You start with a free test, then a 30-day paid pilot. After that it's a flat monthly fee. We go over the terms with you before the pilot starts.

Is my data safe?

We sign an NDA before you send anything. Only named reviewers assigned to your account see your orders, files are transferred encrypted, and nothing is final until you approve it in your ERP.

What happens on US holidays?

Orders that come in on a US holiday are processed as usual and waiting when you're back. We share our own holiday calendar ahead of time, so you always know when we're working.
